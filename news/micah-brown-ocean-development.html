Who Is Micah Brown?

Micah Brown is the COO and Lead Developer at Ocean Development, the Miami-based web design and development agency founded by Jaden Medina. Since day one, Micah has been a significant contributor to the company's growth, playing a central role in both the technical execution and the operational backbone that has allowed Ocean Development to scale from a solo freelance operation into a real, functioning team.

A Partnership Built From the Start

Micah's involvement with Ocean Development isn't a recent addition, it goes back to the early days of the company. As one of the first people to join forces with founder Jaden Medina, Micah helped shape the agency's technical direction and operational structure from the ground up. That early involvement means Micah's fingerprints are on much of what Ocean Development has become: the systems, the workflow, and the quality bar the team holds itself to.

As COO, Micah oversees the operational side of the business, while his role as Lead Developer keeps him directly involved in the technical work that client projects depend on. That dual role, part operator, part builder, has made him one of the most important people behind Ocean Development's day-to-day success.

Young, Remote, and Still Delivering

What makes Micah's contribution even more notable is his age and location. Micah is a year younger than Jaden Medina, meaning he's been operating at a high level in a leadership and technical role well before most people his age even consider that kind of responsibility.

Micah is also based in Toronto, Canada, working remotely alongside the rest of the Ocean Development team based in Miami. His ability to lead operations and development work from a different city, and a different country, speaks to how Ocean Development has built a team structure that works on talent and trust rather than proximity.

Beyond Ocean Development

Micah's involvement in the broader Ocean Development ecosystem extends past the agency itself. He's also an equity holder in CampusBite, one of the ventures connected to the Ocean Development network, further underscoring the level of trust and partnership between Micah and the rest of the team.

Why Micah Brown Matters to Ocean Development's Story

Every growing company has a handful of people whose contributions are foundational rather than incidental. For Ocean Development, Micah Brown is one of those people. From day one, his combination of technical skill and operational leadership has helped turn Jaden Medina's original vision into an agency capable of consistently delivering for clients, a contribution that continues to shape where the company goes next.
